05 · Compliance & Brand Safeguarding

The technical realities most agencies skip past.

AFIS sits at the intersection of education, family, and public debate. That brings platform considerations a generic ecommerce media buyer would not know to flag.

01 · Platform

Meta Special Ad Category

Petition and advocacy content for AFIS is likely to fall under Meta's Social Issues, Elections or Politics category. That removes detailed targeting options, requires a verified Page admin, and limits ad copy claims.

I run Special Ad Category campaigns under live compliance review. This shapes everything from audience build to creative language and disclaimer placement. It is not optional, and getting it wrong risks the ad account.

02 · Brand

Apolitical Tone Discipline

AFIS is explicit about being apolitical and not aligned with any party. Every piece of paid creative is written to that constraint, with a review checkpoint before any spend goes live. No campaign launches without your sign-off.

The same discipline applies to organic. Reactive content on policy news is drafted to inform, never to take a partisan position. This protects the brand long-term.

03 · Technical

Tracking, Privacy & Consent

GDPR, GTM consent mode, cookie policy alignment, and server-side event reliability via Conversion API. Audited at week one, fixed at week two, monitored continuously thereafter.

A clean measurement foundation is the difference between scaling on signal and scaling on noise. For a not-for-profit, every pound of wasted spend is a member benefit lost.

04 · Safeguarding

Children, Imagery & Consent

Any imagery featuring children requires written consent, clear safeguarding policy, and ideally stock-licensed or member-submitted assets with documented permissions.

I work to UK independent sector safeguarding standards on creative selection and will not use any image that cannot be evidenced and defended on request.
